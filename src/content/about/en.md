I build things that run unattended.

Most of my work lives at the intersection of cloud infrastructure and
automation: orchestrating Kubernetes clusters at scale, wiring GitOps
pipelines, and designing systems that stay healthy long after I close
the laptop.

By day I'm a Software Engineer at **Dynatrace**, where the problems tend
to look like "make this petabyte-scale platform faster, more observable,
and harder to break." Before Dynatrace I spent time at Intel on the
Autonomous Vehicle Data Center platform — building authentication and
authorization for the kind of infrastructure that has to work even when
nothing else does.

Off the clock I keep a small fleet of self-hosted services running on
[Mikrus](https://mikr.us) — a hobbyist VPS provider that punches well
above its weight. That includes [OpenClaw](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw) —
an AI agent runtime I've contributed to, plus a private Hindsight memory
layer that ties it all together.

If something I wrote here is useful, fork it. If something is wrong,
[tell me](/#contact).
